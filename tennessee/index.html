How to get a Tennessee personal support services agency license in 2026: requirements, timeline and checklist

Updated October 2026 · Written to T.C.A. 33-2-401 et seq. and Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0940-05-38 · Not legal advice

If you want to open a non-medical home care agency in Tennessee, you need a Personal Support Services Agency License from the Tennessee Department of Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services — Office of Licensure before you take a client. This page sets out what the regulator looks for, in the order you will meet it, and ends with the $1 readiness checklist.

What the license covers

Licensure as a personal support services agency — the four TDMHSAS forms, the $810 fee, the electronic desk audit, the initial, full and provisional licences, annual renewal, expiry and posting; scope of services, the service recipient, the boundary with home health services and Title 68 home care organizations and the exemptions; ownership, the governing body, the person with overall responsibility, reputable and responsible character and the application disclosures; the administrator as the individual responsible for the operation, governance, the organisational chart and delegation; the majority client population attestation and serving only within the licence category; policy adoption, annual review, Exhibit A and staff acknowledgment; sites, one licence per site, counties served, the records address and hours; change of ownership or location, the Change of Status form and surrender of the certificate; advertising and holding out; inspections, access, the Notice of Non-Compliance and Plan of Compliance, grounds, civil penalties, summary suspension, the Licensure Review Panel, hearings, cease-and-desist and waivers; the seventeen-item client record and retention; liability insurance, vehicle insurance, workers' compensation, financial management, closure and continuity of care.

The numbers the rule actually sets

$810 fee per site, 1-year licence, 17 required policies, 10-item personnel file, 17-item client record, 7 competency areas, 7 medication acts, 5 rights, background check every 2 years, registry checks annually, 3 references, 5-year work history, at least 1 unannounced inspection a year — exactly as the texts state them.

What is moving in Tennessee right now

Chapter 0940-05-38 has governed personal support services agencies since 2016, and the 2024 act moved the intellectual and developmental disability majority licence to the Department of Disability and Aging. The rules say what to have — the 17 policies, the personnel file, the client record, the rights — but set no training hours, supervisory interval, retention period or insurance amount, so this manual labels every such figure as the Agency's own. Part D tells you exactly what to verify and what to change when the Department, the rules or the General Assembly move.

The timeline

  1. Set up: form the entity and obtain the certified corporate charter from the Tennessee Secretary of State; obtain the IRS Employer Identification Number; identify the person with overall responsibility and the site manager and gather their citizenship or immigration proof, fingerprint background check and three unrelated references; choose the site, its county, the counties served, the records address and the days and hours; appoint the administrator as the individual responsible for the operation; arrange liability insurance for "the agency, workers and others who provide personal support services" (Rule 0940-05-38-.07(3)) at "[$1,000,000 per occurrence — Agency standard]", vehicle liability insurance where TRANSPORTATION = YES, and workers' compensation and unemployment insurance "[verify Tennessee thresholds]"; decide every option switch and write them into; adopt your policy manual. Do not provide or advertise personal support services before the licence issues.
  2. Manual first: because the desk audit asks for the seventeen written policies, the personnel-file and training evidence and the client-record forms before a licence issues, complete the manual before you file — print Exhibit A with its cover sheet and the mapped policies; the written description of services in the rule's words with enrolment and termination criteria; the organisational chart; the personnel policies and forms; the rights statement, the consumer notice and the fee agreement; the emergency plan. Check that, to 3.08 and MH-4385 Item 12 match word for word.
  3. Regional office: choose it by the county of the site — West, Middle or East — and write it into every form and the cover letter; the office hours are "Monday - Friday, 8 a.m. - 4:30 p.m., excluding State holidays" (TDMHSAS "Become a Licensed Provider" page).
  4. File: the Initial Application MH-4385, the Fact Sheet MH-4386 (ticking "Personal Support Services Agency" under "Non-Medical Home Health", attesting the majority population, "N/A" in items 6 to 22), the Background Check and Non-Criminal Justice Privacy Rights form and the Financial Statement, with the certified charter, the citizenship or immigration proof, the individual-paid fingerprint check and the counties-and-records-address cover letter, to the regional office by post, by hand or through the Formstack web forms; record the date sent .
  5. Invoice and fee: "You will be sent an invoice once the above forms have been received by the Office of Licensure." — pay the $810.00 per-site fee, non-refundable, by the method the invoice states, and keep the invoice copy with the payment record (Rule 0940-05-02-.05(1); Rule 0940-05-38-.04(5);); "All payments must be accompanied by a copy of the fee invoice" (Rule 0940-05-02-.07(2)).
  6. Desk audit: no site visit and no fire inspection — "Initial inspections for Personal Support Services Agencies (PSSAs), D.U.I. Schools, and Non-Residential Office-Based Opiate Treatment offices (OBOTs) are conducted via an electronic desk audit." Send what the regional office asks for from the "ready for the desk audit" set; correct any deficiency it notes — "If the review indicates deficiencies, the applicant will be notified and must correct deficiencies before a license is issued" (Rule 0940-05-02-.06(5)(b)); log every request and response . A denial carries the fifteen-calendar-day Licensure Review Panel request and the statute's further fifteen-day UAPA hearing request (Rule -.06(5)(c); T.C.A. § 33-2-406(f)).
  7. Licence issued: an initial licence for up to one year, for the site and category named — record the licence number, the licence type, the issue date and the expiry date "on the face of the license"; post the certificate "for public viewing in a conspicuous place at the facility or service" (Rule 0940-05-02-.16) and the complaint sign "displaying the Department's regional toll-free telephone number" (Rule -.20(2)) — 866-797-9470 — and record both; put the licence category and number on the consumer notice, the rights statement and advertising.
  8. Staff: for every personal support services worker, the employment application with the five-year work history, three references and employer contacts, the criminal background check with the result in hand before any direct contact, the abuse registry and Sexual Offender Registry checks before direct contact, the signed job description, the worker notice before placement, the orientation and the seven-area competency check-off, the registered-nurse-led medication-assistance training where MEDICATION ASSISTANCE = YES, the written test, the tuberculosis screening, the driver licence and vehicle insurance where the worker drives clients, and the roster entry — then the first client-specific training before the first assignment.
  9. First clients: intake with the service recipient criteria screen and scope screening; the consumer notice, the written fee agreement, consent and the rights acknowledgement signed "prior to the provision of services" and "before beginning service"; the needs assessment and the written service plan; the per-client alternate staffing plan; the medication authorization and information sheet where applicable; daily documentation with dates and times from day one; the first supervisory contact within [14] days and visits at least every [60] days.
  10. Continuing: at least one unannounced inspection a year on a random date; the Notice of Non-Compliance answered by the Plan of Compliance by the date on the notice and the most recent plans kept in a central location; any reportable incident to the Office of Licensure on form MH-5375 and any suspected abuse, neglect or exploitation to Adult Protective Services immediately; the criminal background check every two years and the registry checks every twelve months and before any new direct-contact assignment; annual in-service and the annual evaluation; the semi-annual client quality evaluation; the annual client-funds report where the Agency holds funds; the quarterly self-audit; the annual policy review; the Change of Status form for any change of name, person responsible, site manager, services, hours or counties "[within 30 days — Agency standard]"; re-check the rule.
  11. Every year: when the Department's renewal notice and invoice arrive, file the renewal application with the invoice copy, the population attestation and the documents the notice lists, before the expiry date on the face of the licence — "when a licensee has made timely and sufficient application for a new license (including payment of the required fees), the existing license does not expire until the status of the application has been determined by the Department" (Rule 0940-05-02-.15); no renewal lead-time, late fee or grace period appears in any text — treat the Department's notice as the trigger and [60] days before expiry as the Agency's internal filing date. Expect a full licence, or a provisional licence with a compliance plan, or a denial with the fifteen-calendar-day panel request.
  12. Changes: a change of ownership or of location means "A new application must be made and a new license issued before services are provided" (Rule 0940-05-02-.10); a second site needs its own Fact Sheet, licence and fee (Rule -.09); the certificate is surrendered on revocation, suspension, transfer of ownership or closure (Rule -.17); on closure, assist each client's transfer and keep the records and the consumer notices for the periods in
  13. Public programmes: after licensure the Agency may seek TennCare CHOICES or other managed-care provider enrolment or a Veterans Affairs contract; those programmes set their own requirements (not reproduced) — keep their policies in a separate binder, and remember that a shift in the client population toward an intellectual or developmental disability majority changes the licensing department.

The readiness checklist

Tick every one of these before you apply and before any inspection:

  • Entity formed and the certified corporate charter obtained from the Tennessee Secretary of State; IRS Employer Identification Number obtained; ONE organisational structure chosen on MH-4385 to match the Secretary of State record
  • Person with overall responsibility and site manager identified; their three background and history questions and the felony question answered, with written explanations ready where an answer is yes; five-year address and employment history written out
  • Proof of citizenship or legal immigration status and the individual-paid TBI/FBI (or licensed private investigator) fingerprint background check in hand for the person with overall responsibility and any additional applicant; the Background Check and Non-Criminal Justice Privacy Rights form completed
  • Three references who can attest to character and ability to serve vulnerable populations named, none related by marriage or blood or in a vested business venture with the applicant
  • Site fixed with the full address, county and telephone; the regional office chosen by county; counties served and the records address written into the cover letter; normal days and hours stated
  • Administrator designated in writing as the individual responsible for the operation, with a named designee; organisational chart drawn with the lines of authority
  • Written description of services in the rule's own words with enrolment and termination criteria, the "no home health service" statement and the option switches, matching the service policies word for word
  • Majority-population attestation decided — 50% or more aged and/or with a mental illness or substance abuse diagnosis — and the census worksheet started; Fact Sheet Section 4 ticked "Personal Support Services Agency" under "Non-Medical Home Health"; items 6 to 22 marked "N/A"
  • Exhibit A printed — the crosswalk of the seventeen required policies and the eight personnel-policy subjects under its cover sheet — with every mapped policy complete and tagged, ready for the desk audit
  • Financial Statement form completed; proof of liability insurance for the Agency, workers and others who provide personal support services obtained; vehicle liability insurance where workers transport clients; workers' compensation and unemployment insurance confirmed
  • Initial Application MH-4385 and Fact Sheet MH-4386 completed and signed; filing route chosen — print to the regional office, by post or by hand, or the Formstack web forms; the date sent recorded
  • Invoice expected after the forms are received; the $810 per-site fee ready to pay on receipt, non-refundable; a copy of the invoice to accompany the payment
  • Desk-audit production set ready — the seventeen policies, the personnel policies and forms, the training and competency forms, the client-record forms, the rights statement, the consumer notice and the emergency plan — with a correspondence log opened and the fifteen-calendar-day panel-request clock noted
  • Hiring system ready before the first worker: employment application with the five-year work history, three references and employer contacts; criminal background check record with the result required before any direct contact; abuse registry and Sexual Offender Registry check record; worker notice; signed job description; roster
  • Orientation, the seven-area competency checklist, the registered-nurse-led medication-assistance training, the written test and the tuberculosis screening ready for every worker before the first assignment
  • Client packet ready: consumer notice stating the Agency as employer or contractor and the overtime responsibility, the "no home health service" statement and the complaint line 866-797-9470; written fee agreement and schedule of fees; consent and legal-representative record; rights statement with the grievance procedure and advocacy listing; needs assessment; service plan; alternate staffing plan; daily service record
  • Reporting routes written into the abuse and incident policies: Adult Protective Services immediately with the statewide line verified before printing; the Office of Licensure on form MH-5375 at 866-797-9470, fax 615-401-7644, LicensureInv.fax@tn.gov; 9-1-1 in an emergency; every worker oriented to report directly
  • Licence certificate and the complaint sign ready to post the day the licence issues; licence calendar set with the expiry date, the renewal attestation, the two-year background re-checks, the annual registry checks, the annual training and evaluations, the semi-annual client evaluations, the annual funds report and the insurance expiry
  • Marketing and website describe the Agency only as a personal support services agency licensed by the Tennessee Department of Mental Health and Substance Abuse Services, never as a home health or skilled provider, and appear only after the licence issues

What the regulator reads before you are licensed

Tennessee licenses non-medical home care through its mental health department, not its health department, and the initial inspection is an electronic desk audit of your written policies. This manual builds the crosswalk: every item on forms MH-4385 and MH-4386, every one of the 17 required policies, every personnel-file and client-record item and every requirement of T.C.A. 33-2 and Rule 0940-05-38, mapped to the policy or form that meets it with live page numbers — and every number the rule leaves unset written as the Agency's own and labelled.
