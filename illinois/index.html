How to get a Illinois home services agency license in 2026: requirements, timeline and checklist

Updated October 2026 · Written to 77 Ill. Adm. Code 245 · Not legal advice

If you want to open a non-medical home care agency in Illinois, you need a Home Services Agency License from the Illinois Department of Public Health (IDPH) before you take a client. This page sets out what the regulator looks for, in the order you will meet it, and ends with the $1 readiness checklist.

What the license covers

Governing body, bylaws and the Agency Manager; scope of services, prohibited tasks and service area; licensure, the provisional licence, renewal and notices; inspections, violations and plans of correction; policy development and the rules on site; quality improvement; nondiscrimination; records and retention; office and operating hours; charges and client finances; insurance, contracted services and other laws; branch offices.

The numbers the rule actually sets

5 + 5 hours of training with a competency evaluation after each, 10 hours a year, 90-day supervisory visits, 2 and 10 working days for background checks, 3 months' conditional employment, 7 working days' notice, 2-year retention, 14-day investigation — all exactly as the rule states them.

What is moving in Illinois right now

This manual is written to Part 245 as last amended in March 2025: ten hours of initial training in two blocks with a competency evaluation after each, ten hours a year, and the current Background Check Act deadlines. Part D tells you exactly what to check and what to change when Part 245, Part 955 or the Department's forms move.

The timeline

  1. Set up: register the business with the Secretary of State (and in Illinois, if the owner is out of state — 245.80(f)); secure an office with a telephone, equipment, confidential record storage and fire safety (245.40(a)(12)); obtain the Certificate of Insurance (245.90(a)(2)); adopt the bylaws and your policy manual, designate the alternate and draw the organisational chart; register on the DPH Web Portal and obtain Health Care Worker Registry access (245.90(a)(3)(J)); write your list of services and charges; complete the client contract; recruit and clear your first workers under to 2.05 so that at least one complete file exists.
  2. Apply on the Department's forms with the fee, the Certificate of Insurance, the seven policies, the contract, the acceptance criteria and the sample service-plan forms. The Department will issue a provisional licence within 90 days after receipt of an application that complies with Section 245.90; an incomplete application may be denied (245.100(a)(1)). Do not advertise or provide home services before the provisional licence issues (245.80(b)).
  3. Provisional licence — valid 240 days (245.100(a)(2)). Operate under it exactly as you will under the full licence: the Department inspects within the 30 days before it ends. If you do not substantially meet the requirements but have made significant progress, the Department may renew the provisional licence once for up to 90 days (245.100(a)(4)).
  4. Licence issued; it expires one year from the end of the month in which the provisional licence was first issued (245.100(a)(3)(A)) — enter the date . The initial fee was applied to the provisional licence (245.100(a)(3)(C)).
  5. After licensure: notify the Department within 10 days of any change of name, Agency Manager, address, operating hours, telephone, fax or e-mail (245.80(h);); obtain approval before expanding the service area (245.80(i)); give 30 days' notice of a sale or change of ownership and obtain a new licence before it completes (245.80(d)), and 30 days' notice of closure (245.80(g)); file the renewal not less than 60 nor more than 90 days before expiry with the fee, the annual conviction disclosure and the client load data (245.90(b), (d);) — you may be fined if you file inside 60 days; be ready to demonstrate client activity before your second renewal (245.90(e)). If a notice of violation arrives, the plan of correction is due in 10 days.
  6. Public programmes: after licensure you may apply to the Department on Aging's Community Care Program, the Department of Human Services or the Department of Veterans Affairs as a provider; those programmes add their own standards, and clients served through them are excluded from the renewal client load data (245.90(d)(1)).

The readiness checklist

Tick every one of these before you apply and before any inspection:

  • Office at the licensed address staffed and answering the telephone during stated operating hours; records stored confidentially and fire-safe; operating-hours and after-hours access notice given to every client
  • Current copy of 77 Ill. Adm. Code Part 245 and 210 ILCS 55 at the office; staff familiarised and the familiarisation recorded
  • Bylaws adopted and reviewed annually; policy adoption resolution; minutes; written alternate designation; organisational chart to client level
  • Certificate of Insurance $1 million / $3 million current; Secretary of State registration (and Illinois registration for an out-of-state owner)
  • DPH Web Portal registration and Health Care Worker Registry access in place; Registry log started with every hire logged within 30 days
  • At least one complete home services worker file : notices, Registry check and initiation, result and conditional employment, Social Security card and work authorisation, training record showing five hours and a passed competency evaluation before the first assignment and five more hours within 30 days, task-specific checklist, health evaluation, job description, orientation
  • Competency examination with answer key and the training curriculum covering every 245.71(e) subject area and every 245.40(c)(4)(A)–(P) task you offer
  • Supervisor and trainer qualification records; supervisor reachable at all times; supervisory visit schedule set at 90 days or less
  • Client packet assembled: list of services and charges, contract in at least 12-point type with every 245.220 element and the responsible-party notice, service plan, rights and contacts with the Central Complaint Registry and Adult Protective Services numbers, operating-hours notice
  • Client record system ready; home folder set; acceptance record; termination notice with the seven-working-day rule
  • Complaint, abuse report and investigation, law enforcement and incident forms ready; the 14-day and 24-hour clocks understood by the Agency Manager and alternate
  • Quarterly record review scheduled and the annual evaluation dated; the first quarterly review done as soon as there are records to review
  • Calendar started: provisional licence end date and the 30-day inspection window; licence expiry and the 60-to-90-day renewal window; annual bylaws review; annual training due dates; annual bathing and prescription-shampoo competency; annual observations; Registry annual verification
  • Disaster plan completed; infection control supplies on hand; home safety assessment ready
  • Marketing and website describe the Agency only as a licensed home services agency providing non-medical home services, make no dementia-specific claim unless Option B in is adopted, and appeared only after the provisional licence issued
