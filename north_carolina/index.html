How to get a North Carolina home care agency license in 2026: requirements, timeline and checklist

Updated October 2026 · Written to 10A NCAC 13J · Not legal advice

If you want to open a non-medical home care agency in North Carolina, you need a Home Care Agency License from the NC DHHS — Division of Health Service Regulation (DHSR) before you take a client. This page sets out what the regulator looks for, in the order you will meet it, and ends with the $1 readiness checklist.

What the license covers

Governing body and director, scope and service area, licensure and inspections, policy development, program evaluation, record review, budget and statistics, confidentiality, coordination and referral, contracted services, compliance and the no-smoking rule.

The numbers the rule actually sets

90-day supervisory visits, 48-hour notice, 72-hour complaint investigation, six core skills, Nurse Aide Registry rules — all exactly as the rule states them.

What is moving in North Carolina right now

This manual is written for in-home aide services, which the change doesn't touch, and Part D tells you exactly where the 2023 statute, the unreadopted 13J rules and DHSR's forms may move — and what to do when they do. Older manuals go stale; this one tells you what to watch.

The timeline

  1. Training first. If you have never owned or operated a home care agency, complete the DHSR-approved provider training before you apply (13J .0903(a)); the certificate goes in the packet.
  2. Receipt. DHSR checks the packet for accuracy and completeness and confirms receipt by email (licensure page). The 12-month clock starts: "An applicant must be able to complete all necessary requirements within one year (12 months) from receipt of the initial application and fee date to obtain a license" (checklist and DHSR-4204, page 2). If you run out of time you start again with a new application and fee.
  3. Desk review. DHSR requests your policies, procedures and personnel documents and reviews them against the checklist. Send your policy manual with the C1 crosswalk and the complete personnel files. Expect written requests for revisions; answer each one by changing the policy itself, logging the change and quoting the page.
  4. Initial survey. When the desk review is clear, DHSR conducts the initial licensure survey at your premises (13J .0903(a) — no survey is scheduled before the application is submitted). Under 13J .0904 surveyors identify themselves to the person in charge, must be given immediate access to the premises and records (refusal ends the survey), and may visit clients at home — "Clients will be contacted by the agency staff in the presence of Department staff for permission to visit." Use the Readiness Checklist before you say you are ready.
  5. Deficiencies. If deficiencies are cited, file a plan of correction within 10 working days of receipt (13J .0904(f); Form,); DHSR reviews and responds within 10 working days.
  6. Approval. If no further information is needed, the applicant receives an approval letter and instructions for accessing the licence electronically (licensure page, paraphrased). Post the licence in a prominent location accessible to public view and put the sign with the agency name at the public access door (13J .0903(c)).
  7. After licensure. The licence is for one year and expires at midnight on the date printed; renewal is by application with the $510.00 annual renewal fee (fee schedule). Give DHSR 30 days' written notice before any change of ownership or name or before adding a service category, and notice within five working days of an Agency Director vacancy. DHSR may inspect at any time as a condition of the licence (13J .0904(a)), and DHSR's announcements page asks every provider to keep its site address, mailing address, telephone and email current with the Division.
  8. Processing time. DHSR publishes none. Plan for several months between mailing the packet and the survey, and do not sign client agreements until the licence is issued (G.S. 131E-138(a);).

The readiness checklist

Tick every one of these before you apply and before any inspection:

  • Premises open at the site address on the application; sign with the agency name at the public access door; a place chosen for the licence to be posted in public view (13J .0903(c)); lease and articles in the licence file
  • Governing Body minutes adopting the manual; Policy Change Log started; organisation chart current
  • Agency Director's qualifications documented against 13J .1001(b) (health care practitioner, or two years' supervisory/management experience, or the degree plus one year) with résumé and signed job description; order of authority in the Director's absence written down
  • Service Supervisor (registered nurse) engaged; N.C. Board of Nursing licence verified and the printout filed; signed job description; availability rota during all service hours
  • One complete personnel file for the Director, the Service Supervisor and every aide: application, signed job description, licence verification, Nurse Aide Registry listing (extensive assistance) and Health Care Personnel Registry check noted, competency verification, TB screening, bloodborne pathogen training, Hepatitis B record or declination, orientation, references and signed background-check consent
  • Six core skills signed by the RN for every aide not on the Nurse Aide Registry; for every client who meets the 13J .0901(7) definition of extensive assistance, only a Registry-listed aide assigned
  • Exposure control plan completed with employee risk categories; PPE stocked; post-exposure procedure and provider named
  • Agency emergency preparedness plan completed; blank client emergency information sheets ready for the home folder
  • On-call line working and tested; RN reachable; On-Call Log started
  • Client documents printed: Welcome Packet; Declaration of Home Care Clients' Rights, responsibilities and receipt with the Agency's and DHSR's addresses and telephone numbers, the DHSR Complaint Hotline 1-800-624-3004 / 919-855-4500 and the county DSS Adult Protective Services number; Service Agreement and written statement of services and charges; no-smoking notice; 48-hour notice form
  • Client record system ready with blank forms: Intake, Initial Assessment, Plan of Care, Visit Note, Supervisory Visit and 90-Day Review, Medication Assistance Log, Discharge Summary; record sign-out log; secure storage for originals on the premises (13J .1401(c))
  • Annual budget adopted and statistical records started; retention periods set
  • 90-day client record review and annual program evaluation dated in the calendar; supervision tracker ready
  • Marketing checked against — nothing describes the agency as licensed before the licence issues; the name and address match the application
