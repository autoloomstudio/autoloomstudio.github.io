How to get a Maryland residential service agency in 2026: requirements, timeline and checklist

Updated October 2026 · Written to COMAR 10.07.05 · Not legal advice

If you want to open a non-medical home care agency in Maryland, you need a Residential Service Agency (RSA) License from the Maryland Department of Health — Office of Health Care Quality (OHCQ) before you take a client. This page sets out what the regulator looks for, in the order you will meet it, and ends with the $1 readiness checklist.

The timeline

  1. Receipt. OHCQ holds an application for 180 days from the date of initial receipt; if it is still incomplete then, it is "deemed inactive and administratively closed" and you must reapply. Respond to every OHCQ request promptly and keep a dated log.
  2. Review. OHCQ reviews the application and policies "to determine whether the applicant: (1) Can provide appropriate services to sick or disabled individuals who require care in the individuals' residence; and (2) Meets the regulatory requirements of this chapter" (COMAR 10.07.05.04C). Expect written requests for revisions. OHCQ's 2020 presentation (as summarised, not verbatim) described denial after three unacceptable reviews of revised policies or 12 months without a response. That is not confirmed current: the 06.26.2026 form sets a 180-day administrative closure instead, and the 2026 draft would allow withdrawal after three documented attempts to obtain missing information. Treat every revision request as if it were the last and confirm current practice with the RSA Team.
  3. Inspection. "Before approving or denying an application for licensure, the Department may conduct an announced or unannounced on-site inspection of the agency" (COMAR 10.07.05.04D). If more than one pre-licensure visit is needed because of non-compliance, OHCQ may charge $250 per additional visit or deny the licence (.04K). Use the Readiness Checklist before you say you are ready.
  4. Decision. Approval (with or without conditions), denial (with a detailed letter and appeal rights — appeal under COMAR 10.07.05.28), administrative closure, or withdrawal. A provisional licence of less than a year may be issued in the circumstances in .04J and cannot be extended. The 2020 presentation described a 90-day provisional licence during which the agency implements its policies, admits three clients within 45 days, submits a Statement of Readiness and receives an unannounced survey; confirm the current sequence with the RSA Team.
  5. Licence term. 3 years. Renewal requires the renewal form, changed policies, staff licences/certificates, the annual data collection survey and the renewal fee (.04I).
  6. Processing time. OHCQ publishes none; plan for several months.

The readiness checklist

Tick every one of these before you apply and before any inspection:

  • Office open during the hours stated on the application; once issued, the licence displayed in a conspicuous place at or near the entrance (COMAR 10.07.05.03D); licence file, your policy manual, log available
  • Governing Authority minutes adopting the manual
  • Supervising RN engaged; Maryland licence verified and printed; job description signed; RN's own personnel file complete
  • At least one complete personnel file for each aide you will refer: every item, with dates before the first client referral
  • Orientation delivered including CPR and 3-hour dementia training certificates
  • Skills demonstrations signed
  • Sample client record assembled; blank forms ready in the home folder
  • On-call line working, tested, rota posted, log started
  • Client Welcome Packet, Client Rights Notice, Service Agreement printed with your licence statement, on-call number, OHCQ hotline 1-800-492-6005, your local department of social services' APS/CPS contact and the statewide APS/CPS line (verified)
  • QA Committee scheduled; / ready
  • Emergency Preparedness Plan completed; aide kits stocked with PPE
  • Marketing reviewed against — licence number and the licence statement on everything
