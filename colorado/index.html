How to get a Colorado class b home care agency license in 2026: requirements, timeline and checklist

Updated October 2026 · Written to C.R.S. 25-27.5 and 6 CCR 1011-1 Chapters 2 and 26 · Not legal advice

If you want to open a non-medical home care agency in Colorado, you need a Class B Home Care Agency License from the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment — Health Facilities and EMS Division before you take a client. This page sets out what the regulator looks for, in the order you will meet it, and ends with a free readiness checklist you can download.

What the license covers

Licensure as a Class B home care agency — the Letter of Intent, the COHFI application at least 90 days before start-up, the application contents, the fitness review, the final document review, the zero-deficiency initial survey, issuance, the one-year term, annual renewal with the 60-day trigger, late fees and the hard stop at day 90, and provisional and conditional licences; scope of services — personal care only, the two worker classes, the skilled boundary, the placement-agency distinction, the ten statutory exclusions and the Medicaid/CFC context; ownership, controlling interests, the ten-year disclosure, the CBI/FBI fingerprint checks with the 10-day change clock and the affirmative conviction-disclosure duty; the governing body or designated individual, the manager's appointment, the organisational chart and the fixed Colorado office; the contiguous-counties list, branches, workstations, relocation and territory changes; policy adoption, annual review and Exhibit A; advertising accuracy; changes of ownership, name and address and closure; inspections and the production clocks, the plan of correction, intermediate restrictions and the $10,000 civil fine, enforcement, summary suspension, appeals and waivers; information management, the consumer record, five-year retention and client access; liability insurance or surety bond and the Chapter 2 professional-liability question; fees, the fee-year rule and the 10% performance incentive.

The numbers the rule actually sets

$2,720.30 initial fee (2026/27), 1-year licence, 90-day application lead, 18 task-limitation rules, 3 reporting clocks (1 business day · 24 hours · 48 hours), supervision every 3 months and in person annually, 8+12 manager training hours, 5-year record retention, records to the inspector in 30 minutes, 10% paid back after a clean survey — exactly as the texts state them.

What is moving in Colorado right now

The July 1, 2025 rules fix the eighteen task limits, the supervision intervals, the manager's hours and the three reporting clocks — but set no worker training hour count, service-plan contents or personnel retention period, so this manual labels every such figure as the Agency's own. And C.R.S. 25-27.5 is repealed effective September 1, 2028, with a DORA sunset review before it: Part D tells you exactly what to verify and what to change when the General Assembly, the Board of Health or the fee schedule moves.

The timeline

  1. Set up: form the entity and obtain the Secretary of State organizing documents and bylaws; fix the Colorado office (Ch. 26 § 5.1) with its floor map, choose the contiguous counties (§ 4.2(C)) and the records address; identify every owner with a fifty-percent-or-greater interest (§ 2.17), the manager and any administrator; decide every option switch and write them into Do not provide or advertise home care before the licence issues (C.R.S. § 25-27.5-103(1); Ch. 2 § 2.2.1).
  2. People first: have each owner, manager and administrator fingerprinted through CBI (the individual pays CBI's fee) and prepare the signed ten-year disclosure statement (Ch. 26 § 4.2(D); Ch. 2 § 2.3.3(J);); appoint the HCA manager who meets § 7.2(B) and book the eight-hour Department-approved manager course, with the twelve annual hours diarised; name the supervisor who meets § 7.7 and the Agency's own qualification standard.
  3. Manual before filing: because the fitness review may read the policies (Ch. 2 § 2.7.1(D)) and the survey demands "All required policies and procedures ready for review", complete your policy manual before you file — print Exhibit A with its cover sheet, the Quality Management Plan, the emergency risk assessment and plan, the organisational chart and the scope statement, and adopt the set .
  4. Letter of Intent: submit the LOI Google Form from the CDPHE Letter of Intent page, naming the [COHFI ACCOUNT MANAGER]; wait for the Department's COHFI access instructions (Ch. 2 § 2.3.1; CDPHE Initial Licensure Guidance).
  5. Insurance: bind the liability insurance (or the surety bond in lieu) at Class B $100,000 / $300,000 (Ch. 26 § 4.2(B)(2)) and resolve the Chapter 2 § 2.3.3(D) professional-liability proof with CDPHE named as certificate holder — "[reconcile the two provisions with CDPHE licensing and the broker — Watch]"; arrange workers' compensation and unemployment insurance "[verify Colorado thresholds — out of licensing scope]".
  6. File: build the application in COHFI with the § 2.3.3(A)–(K) contents, the county list, the insurance-or-bond evidence and the attachments; sign under penalty of perjury; pay the non-refundable Class B initial fee ($2,720.30, 2026/27 schedule) by electronic check in the COHFI Payment section — all at least ninety (90) calendar days before the anticipated start-up date; record the submission date .
  7. Answer and wait: respond to every defect notice within fourteen (14) calendar days, watch for the assigned licensing technician in COHFI, and keep communicating — an application can be closed as abandoned after twelve unresponsive months (Ch. 2 §§ 2.3.5(A), 2.3.2(A)); expect three to twelve months overall, with home care agencies "currently taking additional time" (CDPHE Initial Licensure Guidance).
  8. Fitness review: pass it, then freeze the application — any change afterwards triggers a new fitness review (Ch. 2 § 2.7; CDPHE Initial Licensure Guidance); the final document review then collects anything outstanding, including the now-current insurance.
  9. Initial survey: a scheduled call from the HFEMSD survey team sets the date; have ready the five items — manager training proof, employee background checks in the personnel files, all required policies and procedures, the § 13-64-303 acknowledgement and the Quality Management Plan — plus two complete personnel files and the consumer-facing forms; zero deficiencies issues the licence through COHFI.
  10. Open: display the licence conspicuously; deliver the five § 5.6 disclosures to every client within one (1) business day of the start of services — the Department-form Agency Disclosure and Notice of Consumer Rights, the advance-directives information, the payment-source information orally and in writing, and the state hotline in writing "[number — obtain from CDPHE; verify]"; start the service plans, service notes and supervision calendar and the quality calendar.
  11. Operate: screen every new worker, train and validate before independent care, supervise every three months with the annual in-person observation, report occurrences by the next business day through COHFI with the five-working-day investigation report, keep the complaint log two years, and run the annual cycle — policy review, agency evaluation, QM plan approval, emergency-plan review, infection-control evaluation, performance evaluations, task re-proofs and schedule updates.
  12. Renew: application and fee at least sixty (60) calendar days before expiry (ninety for self-insured retention), with the previous year's admissions count and the CAPS attestation; late fees run 10% / 50% / 75% after expiry, and at day ninety (90) the licence is dead and the Agency must cease operation and reapply (Ch. 2 §§ 2.5.1–2.5.3; Ch. 26 § 4.5(B);). A clean, cooperative re-licensure inspection returns 10% of the renewal fee within 60 days (§ 2.13).
  13. Every year and before every print run: re-pull the fee schedule (published by March 1, effective July 1; all 2026/27 figures expire 6/30/2027), check the SOS eDocket for Chapter 26 and Chapter 2 rulemaking, check the 2026–2028 session laws, and watch the DORA sunset review — the whole article is repealed effective September 1, 2028 (C.R.S. § 25-27.5-110) — then re-check every item in Part D.

The readiness checklist

Tick every one of these before you apply and before any inspection:

  • Entity formed with the Secretary of State organizing documents and bylaws in hand; the distinctive licence name chosen so it does not mislead or confuse; the Colorado office fixed with its floor map, and the records address decided
  • Contiguous-counties list written with the staffing assurance; no branch or workstation planned without its notice and fee
  • Every owner (50%+), the manager and any administrator identified; CBI fingerprints submitted and the individual-paid fee receipts kept; the ten-year disclosure statement signed and dated, with case details for any event
  • HCA manager appointed who is 21 or over with a diploma or GED and one year of supervisory personal-care experience (or a degree route); the eight-hour Department-approved manager course booked or completed with the certificate filed, and the twelve annual hours diarised
  • Supervisor named — eighteen or over, experienced or trained per Agency policy, personal care training completed — and designated in writing with availability at all times care is provided
  • Liability insurance or surety bond bound at $100,000 per occurrence / $300,000 aggregate; the Chapter 2 professional-liability proof with CDPHE as certificate holder resolved with the broker; workers' compensation and unemployment insurance confirmed
  • This manual adopted and Exhibit A printed under its cover sheet, every mapped policy complete and tagged, ready for the fitness review and the survey
  • The Quality Management Plan written and approved, ready for the initial survey; the annual agency-evaluation and quarterly record-review calendar set
  • Emergency preparedness risk assessment documented and the eight-element written plan adopted, with hire-and-annual training scheduled
  • The Letter of Intent submitted with the COHFI Account Manager named; COHFI access received and the application built
  • The COHFI application complete with every § 2.3.3 content item, signed under penalty of perjury; the Class B initial fee ($2,720.30, 2026/27 schedule) ready to pay by electronic check; CAPS compliance shown
  • The fourteen-day defect-response clock understood and a correspondence log opened; no application change planned after the fitness review
  • Hiring system ready before the first worker: the Colorado criminal history record check not more than 90 days before employment, the CAPS check and the DORA verification before any direct care, the five-factor assessment worksheet, and the signed job descriptions
  • Training system ready: homemaker and personal care worker training with competency evaluation and skills validation including visual observation before any independent care; the proof-of-competency evaluation before assignment; the task-gated competencies and annual re-proofs tracked
  • Client packet ready: the Department-form Agency Disclosure and Notice of Consumer Rights obtained from CDPHE, the advance-directives information, the payment-source statement, and the state hotline number obtained and inserted before first use
  • Service plan, service note, supervision record, missed-visit record and medication reminder log printed; the no-ambulance back-up rule and the no-pre-filled-notes rule briefed to all staff
  • The three reporting clocks posted where schedulers and supervisors work: occurrences to CDPHE through COHFI by the next business day with the investigation report within five working days; at-risk adult reports to law enforcement within 24 hours by the worker who observed; staff-safety emergency discharges to CDPHE within 48 hours
  • Complaint log opened (two-year retention); the complaint notice naming the Agency, the Department and DORA ready to give to clients
  • Marketing and website describe the Agency only as a Class B home care agency providing personal care services, never as skilled, nursing, home health, certified or Medicare, and appear only after the licence issues
  • Renewal calendar set: expiry date, the 60-day renewal trigger, the admissions count, the CAPS attestation, the insurance expiry and the fee-year of every figure
